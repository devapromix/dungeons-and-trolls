About the Game:

Enter the mysterious Troll Dungeon, a dark and dangerous maze hidden deep underground. Legends speak of the Sword of Dawn, a powerful artifact guarded by ancient magic and deadly creatures. As the brave hero, you must navigate traps, solve puzzles, and face the troll guardians to claim the sword. Only by wielding the Sword of Dawn can you defeat the final evil and win the game.

Tutorial and First Steps:

In this text-based game, you must enter commands after the '>' symbol and the blinking cursor. Start with the command `help` to see a list of all available commands.

The game begins when the hero arrives in the land of Elvion, near the village of Dork. Type `map` to view the map. Try to find the village by wandering around. In the village, you'll find a tavern where you can rest and prepare for your next journey. There are also various shops and a forge. You can sell or buy items to aid you in your adventures.

Now try to find the entrance to the dungeon. Use the command `down` to enter it or `up` to exit. Your goal is to find the Sword of Dawn and defeat the Troll King. The game doesn't end after you destroy evil — you can keep exploring the world.

To view your character's full stats (attributes, health, mana level, etc.), use the command `status`. For all your skills, type `skills`. To show your backpack contents, use `items`, and for equipped gear, use `gear`.

To attack enemies — and yes, you *will* encounter them — use the `kill` command. Combat is automatic. If you can't defeat the enemy, you'll retreat. After battle, you’ll often need to recover with the `rest` command.

You have only one life in this game. If you die, start a new game with the `new` command.
